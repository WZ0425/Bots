## Skills that stay on the PC

Graphify and the Anthropic skill packs stay on the owner PC under `~/.cursor/skills` (Graphify’s rule, when installed, is under `~/.cursor/rules`). They are large and third-party, so this repo does not mirror them wholesale.

The name index in `skills/grok-skill-index.txt` records Grok-side workflow names. Shareable copies in this pack are only the sanitized recipes under `skills/grok/`.
