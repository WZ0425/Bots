# Robotics prior art pack

## When
Need prior art, component category survey, or standards/safety pointers.

## Steps
1. Research question + constraints (budget class, Pi/ROS, workshop).
2. Collect candidates with links/IDs; summarize each in 5–8 lines.
3. Comparison table: performance, cost band, complexity, risk.
4. Kept vs discarded with reasons.
5. What to verify next (datasheet, repo stars ≠ quality, lead time).

## Rules
- Never invent citations; mark gaps.
- Feed recommendations to Systems for architecture lock.
