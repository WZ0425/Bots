# Civil research pack

## When
Need sources, code clauses, paper summaries, or a study pack for a civil topic.

## Steps
1. State the research question and course context.
2. Gather sources via connectors/web; prefer official codes, HKU materials, peer-reviewed or standards bodies.
3. For each kept source: bibliographic ID, 5–10 line content summary, why it matters to the ask.
4. Explicit kept/discarded list with one-line reason.
5. Flag anything Win must verify in Library / Moodle / official PDF.
6. Never invent citations, clause numbers, or quotes.

## Output
Short pack Win can paste or read; PDF if it is a reading deliverable.
